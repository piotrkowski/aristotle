Page-turn strategies

Two-step turns looked fast in a short demo but ghosted in real reading. Aristotle turns pages the way the stock reader does and renders the next page while the panel refreshes.

Measured on the device on 2026-10-17 with aristotle-observe bench pageturn, alternating two full-screen pages of real text with the stock interface stopped. Medians of 6 turns. Two runs agreed within 5 ms. "Visible" is when the first update showing the new page has finished.

Rendering the next page takes about 106 ms on the device, less than the panel needs to refresh.

Binary size

The first build linked fonts and formats that a reader of Polish, English, Greek and Latin books never needs. They were removed step by step, and each step was checked by rendering all pages of a set of test books before and after.

Stripped ARM binaries in MB (106 bytes), built on 2026-10-17 with tools/build-plato.sh and tools/build-render.sh device.
