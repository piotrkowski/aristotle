Page-turn strategies

Fast is not enough: a page must also be clean. Two-step turns looked fast in a short demo but left visible ghosting in real reading, so Aristotle turns pages exactly as the stock reader does, with the next page rendered while the panel refreshes.

How: aristotle-bench --bin pageturn alternates two full-screen pages of real text, with the stock interface stopped. Median of 6 turns; two runs agreed within 5 ms. "Visible" is the first update showing the new page finished.

When: 2026-10-17, on the device.

Binary size

The first build linked fonts and formats a reader of Polish, English, Greek and Latin books never uses. They were cut one measured step at a time, and every page of a set of test books was rendered before and after each step to compare.

How: stripped ARM binaries, MB (106 bytes), built with tools/build-plato.sh and tools/build-render.sh device.
