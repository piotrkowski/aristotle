Your Kindle Paperwhite 4, without the store.

Aristotle replaces the whole interface of a jailbroken Paperwhite 4: the library, the reader and the settings. It is written in Rust with its own toolkit for e-ink, sets books with the document engine of Plato, and has no network code in the reader.

Install Documentation Source on GitHub

Alpha. One device: the Paperwhite 4 (2018) on firmware 5.13.x. The stock system stays installed underneath and comes back when you quit. The interface is in English, with Polish as a translation.

What it does

A library, a reader and settings, built for the panel: every action ends in one update of its final state.

  • Page turns

    One full-screen update with the stock reader's own waveform, so pages do not ghost. The next page is ready before you tap: 0.44 s from release, against 0.57 s for the stock reader.

  • Night mode

    White on black on every screen, switched with one flash.

  • Dictionaries

    Hold a word to look it up offline in StarDict dictionaries. Greek lookups ignore accents and breathings; inflected Greek, Latin and Polish forms find their dictionary form.

  • Footnotes and highlights

    Notes open in a popup. Highlights go to My Clippings.txt in the stock format, so existing tools keep working, and to one Markdown file per book.

  • Folders and series

    Everything in documents/: the book to continue on top, your folders as they are on the device, series in order. Progress from the stock reader and KOReader is picked up.

  • Typography

    Literata, Knuth–Plass line breaking and hyphenation for Polish, English, Ancient Greek and Latin. EPUB, DRM-free MOBI, PDF and TXT.

Upcycle, don't replace.

Millions of older Kindles still work. Their screens and batteries outlast the software Amazon ships for them. Aristotle gives a Paperwhite 4 a second life: a smaller interface that turns pages sooner, on the device you already own.

No store, no recommendations, no account, no telemetry. Books arrive over USB.

Try it

You build Aristotle on a Linux computer and copy it over the USB cable. It needs a jailbroken Kindle with KUAL, MRPI and KOReader.

Changing a Kindle's software can brick it. No recovery method has been verified for the Paperwhite 4, which is secure-boot locked. Try Aristotle only on a device you can afford to lose. It reads DRM-free books only.
