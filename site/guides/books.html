Add books

Aristotle shows everything in the Kindle's documents/ folder. There is no store; books arrive over the USB cable.

What it reads

EPUB, DRM-free MOBI, PDF and TXT; books with DRM cannot be read. Kindle dictionaries and the stock .sdr folders are left out of the library.

As a USB drive

  1. In Aristotle, open the settings (the gear in the library) and set the USB port to Dysk USB (USB drive). The Kindle appears on the computer for 10 minutes.
  2. Copy books into documents/. Subfolders are fine: the library's Folders view shows them as they are.
  3. Eject the drive on the computer.

Without Aristotle running, the stock interface's own drive mode works the same way. While the storage is exported, the Kindle cannot write to it.

Over SSH

With the agent installed and the cable in, copy straight into documents/:

scp -F devloop/host/ssh_config book.epub kindle:/mnt/us/documents/
scp -F devloop/host/ssh_config -r Platon kindle:/mnt/us/documents/

Aristotle scans documents/ when it starts, so new books appear after the next start. The library shows its saved index at once and adds what changed; an unchanged library costs one stat per file.

Folders, series and progress

  • Folders. The library mirrors documents/: subfolders first, then loose books; the back arrow goes up a level.
  • Series. Books whose metadata name a series get a shelf per series, in series order.
  • Progress. Positions from the stock reader and from KOReader are picked up, so a book opens where you left it.
  • Metadata. Title and author come from the book; without them, from the folder and file name.
