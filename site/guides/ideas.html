Ideas

Aristotle reads whatever lands in documents/, and the USB network gives you SSH to the device. Anything on your computer that writes an EPUB can feed the Kindle. None of this is part of Aristotle itself.

A daily newspaper

Turn Hacker News and your RSS feeds into one EPUB a day and copy it into documents/Gazeta/. The library shows it like any folder.

  • A small script can do it: reader-mode extraction of each article, photos redrawn as simple line drawings or described in one line, the top comments of each Hacker News story, and a sync step that keeps the last few issues on the device.
  • Calibre's Fetch news does much the same with its built-in recipes. Save the result as EPUB and copy it over.

Send a web page to the Kindle

Any article becomes an EPUB with percollate or pandoc, and one scp puts it in the library:

percollate epub https://example.com/long-read -o long-read.epub
scp -F devloop/host/ssh_config long-read.epub kindle:/mnt/us/documents/Inbox/

Highlights in your notes

Highlights go to My Clippings.txt in the stock Kindle format, so existing tools (Obsidian plugins, Readwise importers, your own scripts) keep working. Aristotle also writes one Markdown file per book to aristotle-notes/.

Dictionaries for any language

Drop StarDict dictionaries, for example the Wiktionary exports for Ancient Greek or Latin, into the dictionary folder, and holding a word looks it up offline. See Add dictionaries.
