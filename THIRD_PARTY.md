## In the device bundle

`tools/build-render.sh` puts a `licenses/` folder and a `SOURCE.txt` into the
bundle, generated by `tools/bundle-licenses.py` from what the build actually
used. In short:

- **Document engine:** Plato's `plato-core` (AGPL-3.0) with MuPDF (AGPL-3.0),
  jbig2dec (AGPL-3.0), FreeType (FTL or GPL-2.0), HarfBuzz (MIT), libjpeg (IJG),
  libpng, OpenJPEG (BSD-2-Clause), zlib, bzip2, Gumbo (Apache-2.0) and Little
  CMS (MIT).
- **Runtime:** musl (MIT), LLVM's libc++, libc++abi and libunwind (Apache-2.0
  with LLVM exception), the Rust standard library (MIT or Apache-2.0) and about
  a hundred Rust crates, each listed with its licence in `licenses/rust/CRATES.txt`.
  The page snapshots use Zstandard (BSD) through `zstd-sys`.
- **Fonts:** Literata, Noto Sans and Noto Sans Coptic, Libertinus Serif,
  Source Code Pro, Parisienne and Delius (SIL OFL 1.1), DejaVu Sans (Bitstream
  Vera licence), and inside MuPDF the URW base 14 fonts and Noto Serif.
- **Hyphenation patterns** from [hyph-utf8](https://github.com/hyphenation/tex-hyphen),
  compiled by [kl-hyphenate](https://github.com/baskerville/kl-hyphenate):
  Polish, English (US and GB), Ancient Greek and Latin, each under the licence
  named in its master file, which the bundle includes.

`SOURCE.txt` names the Aristotle commit, the Plato revision and patches, and
the exact C library sources, as AGPL-3.0 section 6 asks.

## Not included

Dictionaries, books and the stock Kindle software are not part of Aristotle.
The format notes on the stock reader's files (`docs/library.md`,
`crates/aristotle-library/src/sidecar.rs`) come from interoperability analysis
and contain no Amazon code.
